Paperwork

Read the notice, see what it asks for, check whether you likely qualify, fill the form from answers you learned once, and draft the letter — and nothing is ever submitted for you.

Open Paperwork

Verify every detail against the official source before you submit. This is guidance and a draft, not legal advice.

Status Built and working offline: a pasted notice yields the documents it asks for, the dates, the amounts and the deadlines — each with the sentence it came from; the eligibility conditions are read out of the same text and put to you as questions; three draft templates (covering letter, RTI request, reply to a notice); form answers learned by label and reused; and a local list of applications with their next action.
Release: Windows EXE, Android APK and Linux binary, built from this source in CI — app-latest.
Not built: OCR, so the text is pasted rather than photographed; and the LLM that would explain a document in its own words — the app quotes yours instead, which is why every line it shows can be found in what you pasted.

What it does

  • Finds the documents a notice asks for, and shows the sentence each one came from.
  • Finds dates, amounts and deadlines — every date exactly as it appears in your text, so you can find it again.
  • Reads the eligibility conditions out of the document, asks you about those, and says likely eligible or likely not eligible with the condition that decided it.
  • Drafts a covering letter, an RTI request or a reply to a notice, pre-filled with the documents the notice asked for.
  • Learns a form field once (label → answer) and reuses it on any form that asks the same thing.
  • Tracks applications locally: what you submitted, its status, and the next action.

What it will not do

  • Submit anything. It fills fields and drafts letters; you press the button, at the office or on the portal.
  • Guess a form it does not know. A field with no learned answer stays visibly blank — a wrong answer on a government form is worse than a missing one.
  • Pick between two answers for one label. Both are offered and you choose.
  • Judge a scheme it cannot read. The conditions come out of your document; with none found it says so and refuses to guess.
  • Invent an explanation. There is no model here: every document, date, amount and deadline it reports is quoted from your text.
  • Link out to a portal it cannot reach. The guidance is bundled; the app does not pretend a dead link is help.

When things go wrong

CaseWhat happens
Bad inputAn empty paste is refused. A form with no labels is refused. An unreadable document yields no invented summary. A field with no learned answer stays blank rather than being filled with a near-miss.
No networkNothing changes: the extraction, the templates and the answers are on the device, and there is no portal link to break.
Storage full or unavailableThe answer or draft is not saved and the app says so at once — a half-filled form is never assumed safe.
Corrupt saved stateReported and offered for export, never written over. A partial set loads with the missing labels named.

How to run it

  • Here: play/index.html, or install it from your browser's menu.
  • Offline file: open docs/play/index.html straight from disk.
  • Tests: node tests/self-tests.js — the spec's countable “done means” is asserted there.
