about

The document

Paste the text of the notice, circular, form or letter. There is no OCR here: photograph-to-text needs a model that does not exist in this app, so the text is pasted. Everything the app reports is found in your text — nothing is interpreted.

        Learn an answer

        The app learns the field, not the form: a label and a value. On any form that asks the same thing, the same answer fills it. A field with no learned answer stays visibly blank — a wrong answer on a form is worse than a missing one.

          Fill a form

          Paste the form's labels, one per line. Nothing is submitted: this app fills the fields and stops.
